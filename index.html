Research
Perception, Not Reasoning, Limits Video Spatial Understanding
Jacob Yeung, Mohit Goyal, Florian Dubost, Gary L. Vondran Jr., Federico Tombari, Deva Ramanan, Michael J. Tarr
Website / Paper / Poster

SPLIT is a training-free harness that builds one metric 3-D reconstruction of a video and gives a base VLM tools to query it. It addresses four failure modes (Base VLM → SPLIT):

  • Unseen viewpoints: 43.6% → 76.1% (SpaceCast-Bench)
  • Long videos: 53.2% → 87.0% (HD-EPIC)
  • Never co-visible: 60.7% → 89.2% (VSIBench)
  • Metric measurements: 1.40 m → 0.46 m median error
Reasoning by Consequence: Selective Chain-of-Thought for Driving VLAs
Jacob Yeung, Wenyuan Zhang, Nadine Chang, Michael J. Tarr, Deva Ramanan, Jose M. Alvarez, Pichao Wang, Ke Chen
Website

We show that chain of thought (CoT) improves driving when the car must act and degrades it when the car should keep going. We propose Clutch, a router that selects between trajectories generated with and without CoT, and counterfactual supervision, which trains the trajectory decoder to follow the CoT.

Brain Mapping with Dense Features: Grounding Cortical Semantic Selectivity in Natural Images With Vision Transformers
Andrew F. Luo, Jacob Yeung, Rushikesh Zawar, Shaurya Dewan, Margaret M. Henderson, Leila Wehbe, Michael J. Tarr
ICLR 2025
Paper / bibtex

We propose an efficient gradient-free distillation module capable of extraction high quality dense CLIP embeddings, and utilize these embeddings to understand semantic selectivity in the visual cortex.
Discovery of Linked Neural and Behavioral Subspaces with External Dynamic Components Analysis
Jacob Yeung, Ji Hyun Bak, Kristofer Bouchard
COSYNE, 2023
Paper / Poster

We introduce external Dynamics Components Analysis (eDCA), a linear dimensionality reduction method that finds subspaces of past neural data that have maximal mutual information with subspaces of future behavior data.
